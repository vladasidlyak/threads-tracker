#### Techniques to adopt
- [ ] **Give a reason, not an emoji.** The reply with a reason got 38 likes. Emoji-only replies got 0.
- [ ] **Validate first, then name the pattern in one line.** This is Inés's voice, and it adds value people can save.
- [ ] **End with one specific question.** She never asks back. A question like "¿qué es lo que más te cuesta?" (what is hardest for you?) keeps the thread alive.
- [ ] **Use one short personal line.** Her "firmaba la tarjeta" reply is the model. It feels human, so use it once per thread.
- [ ] **Never side against the woman.** Disagree with the comment, not with her.
- [ ] **Answer "why do I do this?" comments with the quiz.** Do it once, lightly. "Guárdalo para cuando lo necesites 💖" (save it for when you need it) is a good save line.
