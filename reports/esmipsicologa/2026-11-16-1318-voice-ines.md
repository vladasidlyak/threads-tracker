### Voice in one paragraph

### Emojis

- **Frequency:** 19% of posts, 59% of replies.
- **Posts:** the repertoire is tiny and consistent: ❤️‍🩹 ×5, ♡ ×3, then single uses of 📖, 🤢, 🚩, ✨.
- **Placement:** almost always at the end as a soft sign-off, as in "Una vida que poco a poco te ayude a sanar. ❤️‍🩹" and "Los verdaderos privilegios ♡". Exceptions:
  - 🚩 inline after a label ("Red flag 🚩:")
  - 📖 as an opener ("📖 Así es como lo verás mañana", 79 likes, one of the weakest posts)
  - 🤢 as a comic reaction ("...se refiere a mí como “la jefa”. 🤢")
- **Signal:** ❤️‍🩹 and ♡ mean "this is tender, you are safe here". 🚩 is a warning label.
- **Performance:** no lift. Posts with emoji have a median of 841 vs 895 without. Two of the top five posts have none ("Querer a una persona también es trabajarte TÚ para no hacerle daño a ella.", 2187 likes). Treat the emoji as brand texture, not a growth lever.
- **Replies:** emoji-only replies are common (💖 ×8, 👏🏻 ×6, 😍 ×5, 🩷 ×4, 🫂 ×3) and typically earn 1-4 likes. Replies with emoji have a median of 4 vs 26 without, so the text replies are what gets noticed.

### Openings and closings

**Openings (hooks):**
- Repeated: "Hay personas que…" ×3, "Sé exigente…" ×2, "Lo que…" ×2.
- Direct address: "Te pasó porque...", "Tengo una buena noticia para ti:", "Escúchame.", "Nunca olvides que...", "Antes de desvivirte...".
- Label and colon: "Red flag 🚩:", "Algo que me ha cambiado la vida:", "El otro día leí:".
- Universal truth with no framing: "Hay un desconocido que aún se acuerda de ti por un gesto amable que tuviste con él." (1698 likes).
- Weak openers: an emoji label ("📖 Así es como lo verás mañana", 79 likes) and a cryptic sign-off line ("Por algo será. ❤️‍🩹", 179 likes).

**Closings:**
- A short verdict or reassurance in its own sentence: "Tranquila.", "Siempre.", "Y ya estaría.", "Salva el resto de tu vida."
- A soft emoji tail (❤️‍🩹, ♡).
- A repeated beat for rhythm: "Muévete. Más. Despacio."
- Almost no calls to action in posts. The CTA lives in a reply: "En la web de mi perfil... puedes encontrar información sobre terapia ♡".

### Do / Don't for Inés (Spanish market)

**Do**
1. **Speak to "tú" in most posts.** It is the clearest signal (median 1344 vs 664). Model: "Te pasó porque había algo malo en esa persona, no en ti."
2. **Keep the core idea to 1-2 sentences** (40-200 chars). Short and mid-length perform the same (840 vs 895), and "Si tienes que forzarlo, no vale la pena" shows brevity holds up. If you go longer, front-load the hook.
3. **Use permission and reassurance as the payoff:** "Aún estás a tiempo de construir una vida bonita para ti.", "Tranquila.", "Puedes irte."
4. **Use "yo" as a bridge to "tú".** [Conflicts with the competitor's habit: 7% "I" voice, lower median 710 vs 902, about 4 posts, inconclusive. Your brand is personal, so keep it.] Model: "Algo que me ha cambiado la vida: aprender a dejar que los adultos sean adultos..." (1184 likes).
5. **Use a colon hook plus a payoff:** "Sé exigente porque:", "Red flag 🚩:".
6. **Use parallel structures and short repeated lines** for rhythm ("Y puedes irte. Y levantarte...").
7. **Use one soft emoji at the end** (❤️‍🩹 or ♡) as a signature, not a growth tactic (no lift in the data).
8. **If you quote someone, credit them with " - Name" and add one line of your own on top.** Four of the top posts are quotes from others, and we cannot attribute the success to the account.
9. **In replies, be short, human and occasionally funny.** Specific comfort for painful comments, banter for light ones.

**Don't**
1. **Don't state neuro or trauma claims as fact** and don't use diagnostic labels (psicópata, narcisista, "trauma" as a verdict). "Lo que no recuerda el cerebro, lo recuerda el sistema nervioso" is the kind of claim to avoid. [Conflicts with the wellbeing-brand rules: no medical claims, no diagnoses.]
2. **Don't judge or label people harshly:** "...eres una malísima persona" (739 likes). [Conflicts with the warm, relatable voice.]
3. **Don't open with a bare emoji label or post empty images/text:** "📖 Así es como lo verás mañana" (79 likes), the image-only post (223 likes, 4057 views).
4. **Don't post cryptic lines with no context:** "Por algo será. ❤️‍🩹" (179 likes).
5. **Don't post English quotes untranslated** for a Spanish audience: "Asking for help isn’t giving up..." (27 likes, the lowest in the set).
6. **Don't copy signature openers verbatim** ("Hay personas que...", "Sé exigente..."). Create your own recurring frame. Also avoid writing "hay personas que" posts that read as thinly veiled attacks on someone. [Mild conflict: it pushes toward a confrontational tone rather than a warm one.]
7. **Don't use exclamation marks or emoji strings in posts.** Posts have 0% exclamations. Calm is part of the brand. In replies, a "jajaja" is fine.
8. **Don't use Latin American slang** ("funar") or "vosotros/ustedes" inconsistently. Use Spain Spanish.
9. **Don't name real cases or victims for engagement.** Even though "Justicia para Sandra Villar" is in the data (1110 likes), it is a sensitive real-life case and not a fit for a wellbeing brand.

### Rewrite examples

**1. Generic:** "It is important to take breaks when you feel overwhelmed."

> Parar no es rendirse. Si hoy te sientes desbordada, haz solo una cosa y deja el resto para mañana. Tranquila. ♡

*Why:* "tú" address, a one-line reassurance, and a ♡ sign-off, borrowed from "Aún estás a tiempo..." and "Tranquila."

**2. Generic:** "Setting boundaries is a form of self-care."

> Esta semana he dicho que no a un plan y no ha pasado nada. Bueno, sí: he llegado a casa con ganas de estar conmigo. Poner un límite también es cuidarte. ❤️‍🩹

*Why:* it combines a small personal anecdote ("yo", the brand's voice) with the competitor's "también es..." frame ("Permitir que se genere una situación incómoda también es poner límites.") and ends on "tú".

**3. Generic:** "Healing is not linear, so be patient with yourself."

> Que hoy te cueste más que ayer no significa que vayas hacia atrás. Ve despacio, que no hay prisa. Aquí sigues, y eso ya cuenta ♡

*Why:* it uses a permission-style "no significa que..." and avoids any "sanar" promise or clinical claim.
