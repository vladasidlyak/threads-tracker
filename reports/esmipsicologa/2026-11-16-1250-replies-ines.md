### Reply structures that get the most likes

The caveat applies to everything below: these replies sit in other people's threads, so likes partly reflect the host thread's reach. The sample is small (30 visible replies).

- **Own line as a reply (131 likes):** "Las malas personas no se atraen, se permiten." As a standalone post the same line got 206 likes and 10,332 views, so reply context may matter more than the wording.
- **Agreement plus a gentle reframe (108):** "Top. Pequeño recordatorio: ningún partido ni político lo merecen, pero tus límites y tus derechos como persona sí 🩷". It validates, adds a one-sentence reminder, and closes with 🩷.
- **Playful echo of the joke (91 and 43):** "Alguien no? 🫠" and the Squid Game quip. Both are short and self-aware.
- **Shared-frustration reaction (45 and 36):** "Uffff qué pesadilla", "Menuda tela……".
- **Empathy with time and a hug (44):** see the @viurribarri reply above.
- **Pure laughter (67 and 25):** "Hahhaahahahahahah", "Jajajajajajajaajsj".
- **Firm callout (25):** the "vergüenza" reply.
- **Lowest performers:** link redirects, one-word answers ("Siii") and long reassurance (0-2 likes).
- **Pattern in short:** the top replies are short (one or two lines), emotionally clear and light in tone, and they work best when they add one idea or a laugh.
