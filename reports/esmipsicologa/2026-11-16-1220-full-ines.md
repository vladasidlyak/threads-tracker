# Full analysis: @esmipsicologa

Brand: Inés (Spanish market) · Generated 2026-11-16 12:20 UTC · Data: 60 posts and 39 replies (Aug 2024 - Nov 2026), 225k followers

### Summary
- **Short, quotable "truth statements" are the engine.** 55 of 60 posts are plain text, mostly under 250 characters. The median post gets about 750 likes, 105 reposts and 27k views.
- **People share her content far more than they discuss it.** The median post gets about 14 reposts per 100 likes but less than 1 reply per 100 likes. Her audience saves and shares the words as "this is so true", not to start a conversation.
- **Her single best post was someone else's quote.** A quote from Henar Álvarez on women and money got 5,237 likes and 745 reposts. Quotes from Gabor Maté, Nedra Tawwab and Sara Kuburic also rank high. Other people's words, properly attributed, work as well as her own.
- **She barely engages in the comments.** 39 replies in over two years, 11 of them emoji only. This is a clear opening for a brand that actually talks to people.

### What's working
- **Validation aimed at "you".** The top original posts speak straight to a hurting reader and take the blame off her:
  - "Te pasó porque había algo malo en esa persona, no en ti." (125k views, the most-viewed post)
  - "Cómo te comportaste cuando estabas en modo supervivencia no define cómo eres en realidad. Tranquila." (1,620 likes)
  - "Tengo una buena noticia para ti: aún no has conocido a todas las personas que te harán feliz" (3,212 likes, 90k views)
- **Relationship red flags and standards.** "Sé exigente porque: sus valores criarán a tus hijos..." (2,416 likes, 535 reposts) and "Red flag 🚩: sentir que tienes que esconder su conducta para proteger su imagen" (62k views). Posts that give a test or criteria for a partner get shared.
- **Self-check lists.** Her only real list, "Para saber si él es tu persona, hazte las siguientes preguntas" (4 questions), got 1,675 likes and 60k views. The sample is one post, but the format is clearly underused.
- **Longer posts win when they have structure.** Posts of 250-500 characters average about 2,000 engagement versus about 1,050 for shorter ones. Only 5 posts, though, and all were structured (a list, a quote, line-by-line repetition).
- **Repetition and rhythm.** "Y puedes irte. Y levantarte de esa mesa. Y alejarte de esas personas..." (1,157 likes). Each short line is easy to read and easy to screenshot.
- **Tuesday and Wednesday posts average the most** (about 1,450 average engagement), Saturday and Monday the least. The difference partly comes from a few viral posts, so treat it as a hint, not a rule.

### What underperforms
- **English-language content:** a quote from *The Boy, the Mole, the Fox and the Horse* got 27 likes, her worst post. The audience is firmly Spanish-speaking.
- **Promotional or vague posts:** "📖 Así es como lo verás mañana" (book teaser, 79 likes) and "Por algo será. ❤️‍🩹" (179 likes). Without a standalone statement, the post has nothing to share.
- **Abstract body-mind lines without a "you":** "Hay respuestas que primero las siente el cuerpo..." (170 likes) versus the "Te pasó porque..." style above.
- **Image posts:** 5 of 60. The two image posts without text are among the weaker ones. On Threads, her audience wants words.

### How she talks to her audience
- **Rarely, and mostly with emoji.** 11 of 39 replies are just 🩷, 😍, 🫂 or 💖, usually on posts where someone tagged her book or a talk.
- **When she writes more, it's one of three types:**
  - Warm and short to someone in pain: "Está mucho mejor ❤️‍🩹. Dale tiempo… date tiempo. 🫂 Te mando un abrazo inmenso."
  - Firm boundary on her own topic: "La terapia no transforma a un maltratador en un buen marido."
  - A redirect to sales: "En la web de mi perfil (...) puedes encontrar información sobre terapia ♡"
- **Her most-liked reply (2,471 likes) was a quick, friendly answer to a grammar question.** Her other popular replies are jokes on other creators' posts (for example, on @farmacia_enfurecida). Her playful side shows up in replies, never in her own posts.
- **Under her top posts, in a sample of 2 posts and 26 comments, she didn't answer anyone.** Followers write things like "Y dónde están? Cómo las encuentro?" and "A ciertas edades es súper complicado encontrar personas..." and get no answer.

### Gaps and opportunities for Inés
- **Answer the comments she leaves unanswered.** The same questions ("¿dónde están esas personas?", "¿cómo empiezo?") are an open invitation. Follow-up posts that answer a real comment, and replies that continue the conversation, are something @esmipsicologa doesn't offer.
- **Personal story, which she almost never uses.** She has 1 personal-story post in 60, because her brand is an authority voice. Inés can own the "me pasó a mí" angle while keeping the same validating tone.
- **Lists and self-check tests.** These are proven by her best list but used once in 60 posts.
- **Consistency.** She posts in bursts (13 posts in Aug 2025, then 1-3 a month, and only 1 in Aug, Oct and Nov 2026). A steady 3-4 posts a week can take attention in her quiet months.
- **Attributed quotes from Spanish-speaking voices** (writers, psychologists), which her audience clearly shares, mixed in with original posts.

### 5 post ideas for Inés

**1. "No era exigencia, era supervivencia"**
- Format: validation statement, short.
- Draft:
  > Si de pequeña aprendiste a estar siempre pendiente del humor de los demás, no eres "demasiado sensible".
  > Fuiste una niña muy atenta en una casa donde hacía falta.
  > Ahora puedes aprender a descansar.
- Why: it uses the same "it's not your fault" mechanics as "Te pasó porque había algo malo en esa persona, no en ti" (125k views), with a fresh angle.

**2. "5 preguntas antes de volver con alguien"**
- Format: self-check list, 250-400 characters.
- Draft:
  > Antes de volver con esa persona, responde con honestidad:
  > - ¿Qué ha cambiado, además de sus palabras?
  > - ¿Te pidió perdón o solo te echó de menos?
  > - ¿Te sientes tranquila o en alerta cuando te escribe?
  > - ¿Qué le dirías a tu mejor amiga en tu lugar?
  > - ¿Volverías por amor o por miedo a estar sola?
  >
  > Tu respuesta ya la sabes. ♡
- Why: it mirrors her best list ("Para saber si él es tu persona...", 60k views), and structured longer posts average about 2x engagement.

**3. "Me pasó a mí" (personal story, her gap)**
- Format: personal story, 3-5 short lines.
- Draft:
  > Durante años pensé que si me esforzaba un poco más, me iban a querer bien.
  > Un día entendí que el problema nunca fue cuánto daba, sino a quién se lo daba.
  > No fue fácil. Pero fue el principio de todo.
- Why: it pairs the relationship theme that drives her top posts with a first-person voice she doesn't use.

**4. A follow-up post that answers a real comment**
- Format: answer to a question, using a follower comment as the hook.
- Draft:
  > Alguien me preguntó: "¿Y dónde están esas personas que me harán feliz?"
  > No aparecen de golpe. Suelen llegar cuando empiezas a ir a lugares donde te sientes tú misma: una clase, un voluntariado, un grupo de lectura.
  > Empieza por un sitio. Solo uno. ♡
- Why: it turns her unanswered top-comment question ("Y dónde están? Cómo las encuentro?") into content, which builds a two-way relationship she doesn't have.

**5. Rhythm and repetition**
- Format: line-by-line repetition.
- Draft:
  > Puedes cambiar de opinión.
  > Puedes decir que no.
  > Puedes irte antes.
  > Puedes no contestar hoy.
  > Puedes cuidarte sin pedir permiso.
- Why: it follows the structure of "Y puedes irte. Y levantarte de esa mesa..." (1,157 likes, 197 reposts). Short lines make it easy to screenshot and repost.

### Posting rhythm
- **Her rhythm:** irregular bursts. The busiest stretch was 13 posts in August 2025, then 1-7 a month, and almost nothing since August 2026. Posts often go out late at night Spanish time, so they reach Latin America in the evening.
- **For Inés:** 3-4 posts a week, focused on Tuesday to Thursday. Keep at least half as short validation statements, add one list or test a week, and reply to every substantive comment within a day. With this little data, test the timing for 4 weeks before fixing a schedule.
