#### One-liner belief statement (faith-anchored)
> "Make a relationship with God a priority in your life."
❤ 854 · 💬 7 · 🔁 71 · ↗ 8 · 2.4× median

- **Why it worked:** A short, universal instruction with no setup. Readers share it as a value statement.
- **Transferable:** ✅ The shape (one clear instruction, one line) transfers. The faith content does not transfer to Jeff's productivity brand.
- **For Jeff (English, productivity):** Write one-line rules that name a priority, such as "Protect your first hour before you check messages."

#### Short directive with a named priority
> "Put your trust in God."
❤ 780 · 💬 9 · 🔁 56 · ↗ 3 · 2.2× median

- **Why it worked:** Two words of action plus a clear object. Easy to read, easy to repost.
- **Transferable:** ✅ The form transfers. Swap the object for a productivity or relationship anchor.
- **For Jeff (English, productivity):** Try four-to-six-word directives: "Finish the hard task before lunch."

#### Personal confession, hopeful wish
> "I pray you receive an amazing love."
❤ 676 · 💬 25 · 🔁 38 · ↗ 3 · 1.9× median (highest reply rate in the set: 25 replies)

- **Why it worked:** A personal first-person wish that invites the reader to reply with their own hope.
- **Transferable:** ⚠️ Partly. The "I pray" framing depends on the author's faith identity. The mechanic (personal wish, reader sees themselves in it) transfers.
- **For Jeff (English, productivity):** Use a personal wish framed as a belief: "I want you to stop waiting for a perfect week to start."

#### Relationship insight, consistency over confusion
> "When they're serious about you, they'll give you consistency, not confusion."
❤ 642 · 💬 13 · 🔁 51 · ↗ 10 · 1.8× median

- **Why it worked:** A contrast ("consistency, not confusion") that names a behaviour readers have seen. Strong H2 pattern recognition.
- **Transferable:** ✅ Clear, behaviour-based, no fame needed.
- **For Jeff (English, productivity):** This is the template for Jeff's dating and relationships content: "When your plans are real, your calendar shows it."

#### Self-respect boundary, reversed
> "When your intentions are pure, you don't lose anyone, they lose you."

- **Transferable:** ✅ The reframe is plain and universal.
- **For Jeff (English, productivity):** Use the reframe for habits: "When you stop saying yes to everything, your calendar doesn't shrink. Your focus does grow."

#### Relationship expectation, plain
> "What is coming is better than what is gone."
❤ 582 · 💬 12 · 🔁 50 · ↗ 7 · 1.6× median

- **Why it worked:** A short, hopeful line that closes a chapter. Shareable as a comfort statement.
- **Transferable:** ✅ The form transfers. Keep the hopeful tone.
- **For Jeff (English, productivity):** Use it for resets: "Last month's plan is gone. This week's plan is the one that counts."

#### Quote-led relationship line
> "Then one day unexpectedly, you meet the one who shows you how you should have been treated all along."

- **Transferable:** ✅ as a reference, but Jeff should write his own line, not quote this one.
- **For Jeff (English, productivity):** Only use this as a mechanic reference for a standard you set, not a quote to repost.

So what for Jeff (English, productivity): Short directives and one-line reframes win; copy the mechanic, not the faith content.

### 🚫 Hooks to avoid

**Some forms get views but few replies, or they blur into faith or fear.**

- **"I pray…" openers.** Faith-led. Works for this account; breaks Jeff's expert voice. Three of the top 30 posts use it. Skip.
- **Quote-led hooks from other authors.** Two quote posts performed at about the median (514 vs 518). Quoting others adds no reach advantage and weakens the expert POV. Skip.
- **Fear-based "losing" lines.** Only the healthy reversal form ("you lose them") works here. Plain fear or guilt breaks the H3 rule. Avoid "If you don't…, you'll…".

So what for Jeff (English, productivity): Keep Jeff's hooks as his own expert statements, not faith or quotes.
