### 🎯 The 30-second summary

1. **Short, emotionally direct statements win by a wide margin.** Statement posts under 100 characters average about 967 views-to-reactions, versus about 390 for 250-500 characters. For Inés, short, punchy lines about breakups and boundaries should be the default format.

2. **Warm validation and "it wasn't your fault" messages drive the most reactions.** The top posts tell readers that the pain is not their fault or that they are allowed to take care of themselves. For Inés, this is the core promise of her brand: she is the expert who makes her audience feel understood before she teaches anything.

3. **Quoting respected voices and asking reflective questions work, but only in moderation.** Quote posts average about 2.6K reactions, far above the rest, while questions average only about 118. For Inés, use quotes from recognized authors and ask questions sparingly, and only when the question is personal and easy to answer.

### 🚫 What to avoid

- **Links to other platforms or sites.** Posts with links average 35 reactions, far below statements. Keep the link out of the post and mention it in a reply if needed.
- **Repetitive recruitment and promotion.** The @dr.rodrigoarteaga account repeats a newsletter sign-up in many replies (e.g., "Suscríbete gratis en drrodrigoarteaga.com"), and those replies get low engagement. Inés should promote only in her bio or occasionally, never in every post.
- **Long posts with many ideas.** Posts of 250-500 characters average only 390 reactions, while under-100 posts average 967. Cut to one central idea.
