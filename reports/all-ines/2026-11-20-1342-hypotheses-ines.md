### ❌ Rejected ideas

- **Copying the @psico.maxi account-suspension campaign.** The 45× outlier (2.2K likes) came from a suspended account and a 1M-follower community. It is a one-off event, not transferable. Also, asking followers to tag Meta is close to engagement bait.
- **Reply-to-comment giveaways and newsletter pushes (@dr.rodrigoarteaga).** Replies are templated link drops with low likes (median around 15). They are not the persona's voice and offer no expert POV.
- **Dating-style audience questions (@ju_zeppa).** "¿A esta edad todavía se enamoran…?" drew 224 replies but only 169 likes and almost no reposts. This is generic audience bait, which the persona rules rule out. Its replies were also mostly dating solicitations.
