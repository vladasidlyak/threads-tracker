### 💬 How they talk to their audience

**Their replies are short, warm, and mostly one-way. The best engagement comes from the posts themselves, not from conversations.**

- Esmipsicologa rarely replies. Her replies to followers are tiny, like "❤️" (heart) and "Te quiero mucho. ❤️" (I love you so much). Those get 1-2 likes.
- Psico.maxi replies more, and his replies are longer and personal. He answers with "Siento mucho esas dos cosas… mucho… desconcierto" (I'm very sorry about both things… really… confusion), and it got a few likes. He also thanks followers by name, with "🍀" and "Dale, vamos a estar bien".
- Dr. Rodrigo Arteaga's replies are almost all the same: a newsletter pitch ("Suscríbete en drrodrigoarteaga.com"). They get 13-34 likes, but they read as spam.
- Ju_zeppa replies in short, practical bursts ("Si claro", "Eso es genial") and sometimes shares private contact info. That is a risk, not a model.
- Their audience asks questions, and the replies that answer them with a personal line earn the most likes.

#### Techniques to adopt

- [ ] **Reply with a personal line, not just an emoji.** Shows real attention. Works because followers feel seen, and that is why they return.
- [ ] **Answer the question, then ask one back.** Keeps the thread alive. Works because the other person has a reason to reply again.
- [ ] **Use the follower's name when you can.** Feels human and close. Works because it signals you read their message.
- [ ] **Validate before advising.** Acknowledge the feeling first ("Lo que describes duele."). Works because it fits the wellbeing context and avoids sounding like a diagnosis.
- [ ] **Redirect to a safe resource, not a sales link.** Offer a book or a free resource, not a newsletter pitch. Works because it does not feel like spam.
- [ ] **Keep replies under 40 words.** Short replies are easy to read on a phone.

#### Reply templates

- **Thank-you:** "Gracias por compartirlo, me alegra que te sirva. 💛" (Thanks for sharing this, I'm glad it helps you.)
- **Personal story:** "Lo que cuentas es muy valiente. No tienes que tenerlo todo claro para empezar a cuidarte. ¿Qué parte te pesa más ahora?" (What you share takes courage. You don't need everything clear to start looking after yourself. Which part weighs on you most right now?)
- **Question:** "Buena pregunta. Te diría que empieces por observar cómo te sientes después de estar con esa persona. Eso ya te da una pista." (Good question. I'd start by noticing how you feel after being with that person. That already gives you a clue.)
- **Negative comment:** "Entiendo que no compartas esta mirada. Gracias por leer y por tu opinión." (I understand you don't share this view. Thanks for reading and for your opinion.)
