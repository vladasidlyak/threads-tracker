- **Why it worked:** a vivid image plus a "you are the one who pays" reframe. The sale is attached to it, so the emotional core is what spreads.
- **Transferable:** ❌ as written. The sale and a one-off event carry much of the reach.

- **Why it worked:** a numbered promise makes readers want the list. Headline-only, so it earns reach by curiosity alone.
- **Transferable:** ✅ as a format, but Jeff must never post a headline-only teaser. Use the list in the post itself.
- **For Jeff (English, productivity):** "3 habits that quietly drain your focus" works as a hook, but give the three items in the body.

**So what for Jeff (English, productivity):** Build posts as recognisable sequences that end on a sharp reframe, and avoid sales.

### 🧪 New hooks to test

1. **Hidden-cost hook:** "Your to-do list feeds on your attention and walks around looking busy." (Outlier: the feeding-on-your-light post.)
2. **Named-list hook:** "3 habits that quietly eat your focus, and you never notice." (Outlier: three-things post.)
3. **Contrast hook:** "A productive Monday is a plan. A productive Sunday is a mess." (Outlier: saint-outside post.)
4. **Never-does hook:** "A busy person never admits the calendar is the problem. They blame the day instead." (Outlier: never-confronts post.)
5. **Most-dangerous-time hook:** "The most dangerous hour of your workday is the first ten minutes." (Outlier: Sunday post.)
6. **Permission-to-leave hook:** "You can close the tab you never needed. Nothing bad happens." (H3, framed as permission.)

**So what for Jeff (English, productivity):** Test these six in two weeks and compare reposts, since reposts are the reach signal.

### 🚫 Hooks to avoid

- **Blunt label hooks** ("A WOMAN AFTER AGE 40 IS A NARCISSIST'S KRYPTONITE") drew views but few reactions, and break the voice rule against shame.
- **Question-bait hooks** ("Are you this kind of a person? let me know in the comments") got under 60 likes and breach the no-engagement-bait rule.
- **Sales-led hooks** (Black Friday, "link in bio") made up most of the top 5, but are not transferable and fall outside Jeff's funnel.

**So what for Jeff (English, productivity):** Skip shame labels, comment bait and sales hooks; use contrast, lists and recognition instead.
